# Read&Write
Read&Write is a software that provides the ability to have text read out loud for you, among many other features. 

[TOC]
## Reading
There are numerous ways to use Read&Write to read out loud for you, each method depends on the type of program or document you want to read from.
### Reading on Microsoft Word
Click anywhere in a sentence of your choice you would like to start reading from, and press **Play**. 
Read&Write will begin reading that sentence, and will continue to read the entire document until you press **Stop**
![Reading from Word](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1767953517/Reading_from_Word_ic70b5.gif)
### Reading on the Browser
Highlight a sentence you would like to start reading from, and press **Play**.
The entire web page from that sentence onwards will be read out, until you press **Stop**.
![Reading from Browser](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1767955051/Reading_from_Browser_kgos4k.gif)
### Reading from PDFs 
Using Read&Write to read PDF's isn't as straightforward as the other methods, as Read&Write can't read from PDF's natively. There are however a few methods available to read a PDF out loud.
#### Method 1 - PDF Reader ![PDF Reader](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1767956494/Screenshot_Reader_istbwj.png)
Read&Write has a PDF Reader allowing you to read PDF's.
##### Setup
1. Download the PDF you wish to read from.
2. Click on the **PDF Reader** in the Read&Write ribbon.
3. Choose the PDF in your file explorer, and click **Open**.
![Selecting PDF in PDF Reader](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1767957292/Selecting_PDF_Reader_hqtskn.gif)
##### Using PDF Reader
Click anywhere in a sentence you would like to read from.
Read&Write will begin reading from that sentence automatically, and will read out the entire document unless you press **Stop**.
![Demonstrating PDF Reader](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1767967855/Demonstrating_PDF_Reader_sqjz1c.gif)
#### Method 2 - Screenshot Reader ![Screenshot Reader](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1767969240/Screenshot_Reade_ajfgsy.png)
To use the screenshot reader, click on the **Screenshot Reader** icon, and then click and drag your mouse around the text you wish to have read out.
!!! info The screenshot reader can only read out what you have selected.

To stop from reading, you can either press stop on the Read&Write ribbon, or the stop button that appears on the selected area.
![Demonstrating Screenshot Reader](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1767969255/Demonstrating_Screenshot_Reader_lioepg.gif)
The screenshot reader also pins the selected area on the screen, to close it, click the "X".
!!! note Read Text Anywhere on Your Screen
	The screenshot reader can also read from images, or any other text which is otherwise unselectable.
	![Demonstrating Screenshot Reader with Images](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1767970108/Screenshot_Reader_Images_lo4zmt.gif)

## Reading from Physical Documents or Inaccessible/Unselectable Documents
Read&Write also provides the ability to read pages from a physical document such as a book. This requires a printer/scanner. 
### Scan ![Scan Tool](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1768215581/Scan_qp2r2a.png)
The scan tool provides the ability to convert physical documents such as books, or unselectable documents into a word or PDF file.
#### Using a Printer/Scanner ![Printer/Scanner](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1768216390/Scan_Tab_mwtwns.png)
!!! warning I don't have a printer.
	I don't have a printer, so this isn't going to be very intuitive. 
1. Place the document face down on the scanner.
2. Ensure that the printer/scanner is connected and Read&Write has detected your device under **Select your device**
![Select Device](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1768217155/Scan_-_Select_Device_rt513v.png)
3. Choose whether you want to convert the document into a PDF or Word Document.
![PDForWord](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1768217155/PDF_or_Word_o0xjje.png)
4. Choose whether you want to scan a **single** page or **multiple** pages.
![Single or Multiple Pages](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1768217155/SingleMultiple_ss8ykp.png)
Now I will give you directions for both scanning a single page or multiple pages.
##### Single Page
If you followed the previous steps and the document is correctly lined up on your scanner, press **Scan**.
##### Multiple Pages
Press **Scan** for the first page. Line up the second page on the scanner, press **Scan** again.
When finished scanning all the pages you require to convert, press **Finish**.
#### Scan From File ![Scan from File](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1768219086/Scan_From_File_bqtwt7.png)
If you already have scanned documents, or come across a PDF where the text isn’t selectable, Read&Write can convert it into an accessible document with selectable text using OCR. The supported file types can either be any image, or PDF.
I will be using [this article](https://www.academia.edu/download/41211508/00463531d9d5ba131f000000.pdf20160115-19908-11dun8g.pdf) as an example where all pages had been scanned in.
1. If you don't already have access to the document, download it.
2. Drag the file where it says **Drag and drop** or press **Select File(s)**
3. Choose which pages you wish to convert. Leave default if you'd like to convert every page.
![Selecting Document and Pages](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1768220329/Scan_Drag_and_Drop_asxs84.gif)
4. Choose whether you want to convert to a PDF or Word Document.
4. Press **Scan**.
Read&Write will open up the file explorer where you choose to save the converted document.

##### Finished Product
If you chose **PDF**, Read&Write will open it's own PDF Reader. 
If you chose **Word**, Read&Write will open up the converted document in a word file. 
The formatting of the document as well as images will be the same as the original document.
And here is the final product:
![Scan from File End Product](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1768221227/Scan_From_File_End_Product_jtihix.gif)
## Writing
Read&Write isn't just for helping you with reading, but it can also help you with writing too. 
### Check It ![Check It Symbol](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1768228951/Check_It_yjlnm6.png)
Check It is a spellchecker provided to you by Read&Write. Here's how to use it:
1. Click on the document you wish to check the spelling for.
	You can also highlight an area of text where there might be some spelling errors too.
2. Click **Check It** on the Read&Write ribbon.
#### Check It Rundown
When you click on **Check It**, the Check It window will appear:
![Check It Screenshot](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1768230161/Check_It_Rundown_mzqq65.png)
Let's go over all it's features according to the numbered elements in the picture:
1. Shows you the context of the spelling error, to help you recognise what word to replace the mispelled word with.
2. Select the correct suggestion the word is supposed to be.
3. Shows you the definition of the word you have selected.
4. If you wish to correct the word, press the green indicated button to accept the spelling correction. 
	If there are more than one spelling, Read&Write will move to the next word.
5. If Read&Write is recognising the word as a misspelling when it actually isn't, press this button to add the word to Read&Write's dictionary so the word doesn't get flagged as incorrect again.
6. Allows you to ignore the word without correcting it, and adding to the dictionary.
7. Allows you to skip the word and go to the next misspelling.
### Prediction ![Prediction](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1768233184/Prediction_uvphtv.png)
**Prediction** brings predictive text to your computer. It can help you write faster, and help you with your spelling. To use it:
- Click on **Prediction** to activate it.
A window that looks like this will appear:
![Prediction](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769185438/2026-01-23_16_23_12-Document5_-_Word_tifwgn.png)
Now as you type, Read&Write will predict the word you are about to write.
![Prediction Demonstration](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769185588/2026-01-23_16_25_48-Document5_-_Word_p9tumy.png)
You can use this feature to speed up your writing speed, if you see the word you're about to write in the suggestions, you can either click on the word to insert it, or use one of the indicated F Keys next to the word.
### Dictionary ![Dictionary](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769609473/Dictionary_as5fwe.png)
The Dictionary allows you to get definitions for words on the go.
There are two ways of using it:
#### Method 1 - Definition for Selected Word
1. Select the word you wish to define.
2. Press the **Dictionary** button.
3. Make sure to enable **Advanced** in the Dictionary pop-up.
![Dictionary Demonstration](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769604152/ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter_8_neo8x3.gif)
#### Method 2 - Searching for a Word
1. Click **Dictionary**.
2. Search for the word in **Enter a word**.
3. Press **Enter** or click on the **Search Icon**.
4. Remember to click **Advanced** to ensure the definition you want shows.
![Dictionary Method 2 Demonstration](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769608561/Dictionary2-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter_ile2wi.gif)
!!! info Definitions for various words are missing.
	If you can't get a definition of a certain word, click **Web Dictionary**.
	Read&Write will provide the definition using Google.
	![Web Dictionary](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769609214/Web_Dictionary_ovzmpw.gif)
### Research Folder ![Research Folder](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769680849/Research_Folder_dvk7i1.png)
The Research Folder is a powerful way to organize and manage research from multiple sources in one place. It works similarly to highlighting, but instead of leaving notes scattered across pages, all your research is saved in a single folder you can access anytime. Each entry keeps its source, making it easy to revisit the original paper whenever you need.

When you click on the Research Folder, there are three buttons to choose from:
![Research Folder Expanded](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769680835/Research_Folder_-_Expanded_er5qnx.png)
#### Add Text ![RF - Add Text](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769680869/RF_-_Add_Text_hfol6r.png)
This button allows you to collect snippets from various sources to add to your research folder.
Highlight some text you would like to save for later, and then press **Add Text**.
	![RF - Add Text](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769685542/RF_-_Add_Text_v8ofti.gif)
!!! info For PDF's, you need to copy the text you've highlighted before clicking on **Add Text**.
A new window will pop up called **Note Detail**. 
	![RF - Add Detail](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769686703/RF_-_Add_Detail_ugg07o.png)
- **Title** - To make it more identifiable, you can give the note you just saved a title.
- **Date** - Is autofilled to today's date, but you can use this field to keep track of when the article/web page you're using was published.
- **Author** - Autofilled based on your computer's username, but you can use this field to keep track of the author of the website/article.
- **Sources** - If you're acquiring research from the browser, this field should be filled in automatically based on the page's URL.
	This is the field that will help you keep track of where acquired the note from.
!!! info Sometimes the source doesn't get autofilled, and you may need to copy the URL of the page into **Sources** manually.
- **Folder** - This allows you to categorise the note, which will then give you the ability to filter all your notes based on the folder you put your note in.
	**To create or add your research to an existing folder**: 
		- Click on the button that looks like this: ![Folder Button](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769691587/23e9f811-32cf-475b-b1b7-5abe0b40fbef.png)
		- A new window will pop up:
		![Select Folders](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769691706/e670b084-a3ab-467e-bc5b-4c74efe9b6a4.png)
		- To create a new folder, click **Add**, enter a name for the folder and then click **Ok**.
		![Edit Folder Name](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769691984/fca42a8f-87c8-48b1-8b41-d26f94d5b21e.png)
		- To add the note to a folder you just created, click on the **checkbox** next to it, then press **Ok**.
- **Comment** - Add a comment based on the note you are saving.
Once finished, press **Save**.
!!! warning Every time you save some research, a new window will pop up that says *Your text has been added to your Research Folder*.
	For you to save some more text into the research folder, you need to make sure to press **OK** on this window, otherwise Read&Write won't allow you to save more research.
	![Read&Write Confirmation](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769692291/e88d9e8f-d99c-4775-8b94-4b28c7b59aa5.png)
#### Add Image ![RF - Add Image](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769680861/RF_-_Add_Image_jr5kmv.png)
You can use this feature to save images, or text that may not be selectable.
- Click on **Add Image**.
- You will be able to save an area of your screen to the research folder.
![RF - Add Image](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769696327/fa452e13-0abf-40df-b4c5-201496aa0f8b.png)
#### Research Folder ![Research Folder](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769680881/RF_-_Reading_Folder_tig22m.png)
The **Research Folder** displays all of the research you collected.
![RF - Research Folder](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769699130/0f5db56c-df38-4d83-96b5-3b583bfbb7ee.png)
- **All** shows every note. Selecting a folder filters the list to only the notes saved in that folder.
- Clicking on a note in the list allows you to see the content of that note under **Content**.
- Double-click a note to edit it and view its source, allowing you to revisit the page where the note originated.
### Screen Masking ![Screen Masking](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769700819/7db66877-552d-4868-9b66-68ecb011aee6.png)
Screen Masking applies a customizable colored overlay to your screen. By default, it uses a soft green tint, but you can adjust the color to suit your preference.
Use Screen Masking to make reading more comfortable if black text on a white background feels overwhelming, reduce eye strain, or apply it as a blue light filter.
![Screen Masking Demonstration](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769701343/834e1251-5fe4-4de5-95fd-0f1d687bc974.png)
**To change settings:**
Click on the **settings cog** on the small window that appears when you activate Screen Masking
![SM - Access Settings](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769701582/00047467-ce0c-4e13-95da-a0554e1d18f3.png)
#### Screen Masking Settings
At the top of the Screen Masking settings, you can choose the type of screen masking you want to use. There are several options available, allowing you to select the one that works best for you. 
![Top of Screen Masking Settings](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1769701925/d7f37953-7033-4389-9f6c-e6838bfd4294.png)

#### Reading Ruler
The Reading Ruler is a helpful tool designed to keep track of the line you’re reading. You can customize its color in the settings, and adjusting the **Reading Ruler Height** allows you to change the thickness of the ruler. This can make it easier to accurately follow along and pinpoint your place while reading.

### Highlighter ![Highlighter](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1770039858/a9002d46-d661-4e4e-94a0-91741ca2de29.png)
Read&Write also comes with a highlighter with multiple colour options.
Select the text you wish to highlight, and then select the highlight colour to highlight.
![Highlight](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1770044154/Highlight_qijgtk.gif)
If you're highlighting text on a word document, Read&Write uses Word's **fill colour** functionality to highlight the word. You can save the document and the highlights too will be saved.

You can highlight any text that is selectable. The highlights of word documents can be saved, but anywhere else such as a web-page, the highlights will disappear after you refresh the page, or close the window.

To save highlights, Read&Write comes with a feature called **Collect Highlights**![Collect Highlights](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkiv1r3m7/image/upload/v1770042385/f1dca017-8dcf-48c4-acb3-cc926cf172c5.png).

With **Collect Highlights**, Read&Write will save all the highlights you have made on a page and group all the highlights based on the colour you used to highlight the text.
